Privacy Policy

App: Kinder Kids (the “App”)
Developer: MA DevStudio (“we”, “us”)
Contact: ahmer1246@gmail.com
Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Our promise

The App is a learning app for children (English letters, words, reading books, stories, numbers and shapes). We designed it to work without accounts and without collecting personal information from children or parents.

This policy explains what the App does and does not do, and it follows the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Information we do NOT collect

We do not ask for, collect, store on our servers, or share:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses or birth dates;
  • photos, videos, voice recordings or files (the App does not use the camera or microphone);
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, calendar or other data from your device;
  • chat messages, user-generated content or social media information;
  • the Android Advertising ID (the App does not request the AD_ID permission).

The App has no sign-up, no login and no social features.

3. Information stored on your device only

To make the App work, the following is saved only on your device and never sent to us:

  • learning progress (stars, completed lessons and phases);
  • whether Premium is active on this device;
  • app settings.

Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

4. Advertising

The free version may show ads from Google AdMob. We have set AdMob to child-directed treatment and under-age-of-consent, we request only non-personalized, G-rated ads, and we do not use interest-based or behavioral advertising. Ads are never shown during a lesson question, and Premium removes ads.

To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may process limited technical information such as device type, operating system, IP address and the ad shown, in line with child-directed settings. Please see Google’s information on how it uses data in apps: policies.google.com/technology/ads

5. Purchases

Premium (subscription or one-time purchase) is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card, bank or payment details. Google Play may share purchase status (for example, that Premium is active) with the App so we can unlock content. Purchases and settings are protected by a parental gate (a simple question for adults).

6. Voice and speech

The App uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine to read words aloud. Text is spoken on your device’s speech service. The App does not record or listen to the child.

8. Permissions

The App may request only: Internet / network state (to show ads and process purchases) and Billing (for Premium). It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

9. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information, there is nothing to review, change or delete on our side. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Parents’ choices

Parents can: use the App without ads by choosing Premium; manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions); and ask us any privacy question using the contact details below.

11. Data security

Progress data stays on the device and is protected by the device’s own security. We keep no child data on servers, so there is no child data for us to lose or sell. We never sell or rent personal information.

12. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data. Local app data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.

13. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, we hold nothing to provide, but you may contact us at any time.

14. Links

The App does not contain links to other websites for children. Links in the Parent Zone (for example, Google Play subscription management) sit behind the parental gate and are governed by the policies of those sites.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example, by adding a new service), we will update the date at the top and, when the change is important, show a notice in the App. Please review it from time to time.
